FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the strike data come from?

From the public strike calendar of the Commissione di Garanzia Sciopero (CGSSE), collected by the onData project. Oggi Sciopero is an independent app and isn't affiliated with the Commission or any transport operator. Always check with your operator before you travel.

Why don't I see a strike in my city?

Make sure the region is selected in Settings. You see local strikes for the regions you follow, plus nationwide and general strikes. If a strike is missing, it may not be in the CGSSE calendar yet.

What are the guaranteed service windows?

Italian law requires transport to run normally during certain hours of a strike day, the fasce di garanzia. The day timeline shows these windows next to the strike hours. Times can differ by operator, so check the official text for details.

A strike was called off. Does the app show that?

Yes. Revoked strikes are flagged in the app and stay visible for a few days, so you can see that the strike is no longer happening.

I'm not getting reminders. What should I check?

Make sure notifications are allowed in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Oggi Sciopero; on Android: Settings → Apps → Oggi Sciopero → Notifications), and that reminders are turned on in the app. Reminders are scheduled for strikes in your regions and on your lines, at the times you choose.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The latest strike calendar is saved on your phone. Connect to the internet now and then to get new strikes.
