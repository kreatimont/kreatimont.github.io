Oggi Sciopero

Is there a strike today? The calendar of transport strikes in Italy, for iPhone and Android.

Overview Support Privacy Policy

About

Know before you travel

Oggi Sciopero is the calendar of transport strikes in Italy: trains, metro, buses, trams, flights, ferries and taxis. See the strike hours and guaranteed service windows for the regions you travel in, check your metro lines, and get a reminder the evening before.

Features

What's Inside

📅

Today's & Upcoming Strikes

Open the app and see right away whether there's a strike today and what's coming up. Each strike shows the sector, the company, the hours and the area.

📍

Your Regions

Pick the regions you care about and see their local strikes together with nationwide ones. General strikes are always included.

🚇

Your Metro Lines

Follow metro lines in Milan, Rome, Naples, Turin, Genoa, Brescia and Catania: M1, Line A and all the others. Each line shows the status of the company that runs it, so you know at a glance if it runs.

🕒

Strike Hours & Guaranteed Service

The day timeline shows strike hours and the guaranteed service windows (fasce di garanzia). The official text is one tap away.

🗺️

Strike Map

A detailed map highlights the affected areas, region by region.

🔔

Reminders

Strikes must be announced at least 10 days ahead, so there's time to plan. Get a reminder the evening before and the morning of a strike, at times you choose.

↩️

Revoked Strikes

Strikes can be called off at the last minute. The app flags revoked strikes and keeps them visible for a few days.

📴

Works Offline

The latest update stays saved on your phone, so you can check it without a connection.

Data

Where the data comes from

Strike data comes from the public calendar of the Commissione di Garanzia Sciopero (CGSSE), collected by the onData project. Oggi Sciopero is an independent app and isn't affiliated with the Commission or any transport operator. Always check with your operator before you travel.
