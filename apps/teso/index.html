Teso: Couples Games

Date night fun for two. Private games for every mood, with no account needed.

Overview Support Privacy Policy

About

Pick a game and play together

Teso is a collection of games for couples. Open the app, pick a game and play together on one phone: on the couch, on a date or on a long drive.

Keep it light and silly on a lazy evening, ask deeper questions when you want to feel closer, or pick a fresh date idea when you want to do something new.

Features

14 games, 1,000+ cards

Everything you need for a night in, with no sign-up first.

☀️

Game of the Day

Teso picks a game for you every day, so you can skip the deciding and start playing. It's free every day.

🎲

Couples Classics

Never Have I Ever, Would You Rather, Truth or Dare and This or That, all written for two.

🃏

10 Swipe Decks

Swipe right on the ideas you both love and get a list of matches. Decks include Romance, Date Ideas, Deep Talk, Memory Lane and Travel & Countries.

🌙

For Every Mood

Choose what suits your evening, from silly and playful to slow, cozy and deep.

✨

Suggested for You

Get new suggestions every day, tap Surprise Me when you can't choose, and keep a streak going on the days you play.

🔒

Private by Design

No accounts and no sign-up. Your play history stays on your phone, and you can play offline.
