Support

Have a question or need help with Teso? Find answers below or reach out directly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need an account or two phones?

No. Teso has no accounts or sign-up, and you play together on one phone.

What's free, and what does Teso Premium include?

The Game of the Day is free every day. Teso Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks every game and every deck.

Does Teso work offline?

Yes, the games work offline. You need an internet connection to buy or restore a subscription.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your play history stays on your device. Teso has no ads or analytics. It uses RevenueCat only to manage subscriptions. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchases in Teso's settings or paywall. Your play history isn't synced by Teso, so it only moves across if it's included in a device backup you restore.

How do subscriptions work and how do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel, go to your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You'll keep access until the end of the billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

There's no account to delete. Deleting Teso removes its local data from your iPhone.

I had an account in an earlier version. What happened to it?

The current version of Teso doesn't use accounts, partner sync or AI conflict analysis. To request deletion of your old account data, email nadtoka.o.work@gmail.com from the address you used for that account.
