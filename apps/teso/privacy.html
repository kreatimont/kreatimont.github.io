Privacy Policy

Applies to: Teso: Couples Games

Effective Date: November 5, 2026  ·  Developer: Alexandr Nadtoka

Teso: Couples Games ("the App") is a collection of games that couples play together on one phone. The App has no accounts and no backend of its own. This policy explains what stays on your device, the limited data used to handle subscriptions, and the choices you have.

1. The Short Version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email address to use the App.
  • Your play history is stored on your device. We can't see it.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics and no cross-app tracking.
  • The App uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, as described in Section 3.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps the following data locally on your device:

  • Play history: the days you played and your most recently played games, which are used for features such as your streak and suggestions.
  • App state: whether you have completed onboarding and a cached copy of your premium status.

We have no access to this data. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device settings. Those backups are governed by your settings and, for iCloud, by Apple's terms.

3. Subscriptions and Third-Party Services

The games themselves work without an internet connection. The App connects to the services below to offer, verify and restore subscriptions. These checks can happen when you open the App, even if you haven't subscribed.

Provider Purpose Data involved
RevenueCat, Inc. Subscription management and purchase verification A random anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information, and basic technical information such as app version, device and operating system details, region, and IP address. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
Apple Inc. App Store payments and optional crash reports Apple processes your payment, and we never see your payment details. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and diagnostic reports. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not link your subscription information to your name or email address. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. When you start an email from the App's settings, the draft includes your app version, iOS version and device type to help us diagnose problems. You can review or remove these details before sending. We use this information only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose and our records.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data on your device: deleting the App removes its local data from your device. Copies in existing device backups are handled according to your backup settings.
  • Subscription records: RevenueCat and Apple keep these according to their own policies and legal obligations. To request deletion of RevenueCat records linked to your purchase, contact us and include your purchase details so we can try to find them, since the App has no account to look up.
  • Subscriptions: deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. You can cancel in your App Store account settings.

6. Earlier Versions of Teso

Earlier versions of the App offered account-based features, including partner sync and AI conflict analysis, that relied on cloud services. The current version does not use those accounts or features. If you had an account and would like your data deleted, email nadtoka.o.work@gmail.com from the address you used for that account, or tell us the name and email shown on it.

7. Age Requirement

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to restrict or object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at nadtoka.o.work@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing and verifying your subscription), and legitimate interests (responding to support requests and keeping the App working reliably). RevenueCat is based in the United States, so subscription data may be processed there under the safeguards described in its policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post any changes here with a revised effective date. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will also notify you in the App.
