Support

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is City Transport available on Android?

Yes. City Transport is available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. The Android version currently covers Ukrainian cities.

Which cities are supported?

On iPhone, City Transport covers 70+ cities across 15 European countries, including 40+ cities in Ukraine (Kyiv, Lviv, Kharkiv, Odesa, Dnipro) plus Rome, Warsaw, Berlin, Amsterdam, Prague and more. On Android, it covers 44 Ukrainian cities and the Zakarpattia, Ivano-Frankivsk and Cherkasy oblasts. The full list is in the App. If your city is missing, email me.

The map isn't showing any vehicles. What should I check?

Make sure you have an active internet connection and that at least one route is selected. Try the manual refresh button. If the issue persists, the city's transit data service may be temporarily unavailable. Let me know which city and route, and I'll take a look.

How often does vehicle data update?

You can set the update interval in Settings, or switch to manual mode and refresh only when you tap the refresh button.

Do I need to allow location access?

No. Location access is optional. If you allow it, the App uses it only to show you on the map and center the map on you. All features work without it.

How do I remove ads?

On iPhone, subscribe to City Transport Pro in Settings. It removes ads and unlocks extra features. The Android version doesn't offer a way to remove ads yet.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription on iPhone?

To restore, open Settings in the App and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → City Transport.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can review your ad consent from the App's settings. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Google or privacy settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I switch the interface theme?

Open Settings in the App and pick light, dark, or the system theme that follows your phone's appearance.
