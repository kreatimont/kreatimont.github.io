Privacy Policy

Applies to: City Transport for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play)

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

City Transport ("the App") is developed and maintained by Alexandr Nadtoka ("I", "me", "my"). The App shows public transport vehicles live on a map. This Privacy Policy describes what data the iOS and Android versions of the App use and how it is handled. Where the two versions differ, this is noted below.

In short: the App has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. It shows ads and uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Your favorites and settings stay on your device.

Location Data

With your permission, the App uses your device's location to show you on the map and center the map on you. Location access is optional: the App works fully without it.

  • Location is used only while the App is open. It is never used in the background.
  • Your location is not stored and not sent to me or to transit data providers.
  • On iPhone you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → City Transport.
  • On Android you can change this in Settings → Apps → City Transport → Permissions → Location.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following locally, on your device:

  • Your settings: city, theme, language, update interval and map preferences.
  • Your favorite routes and stops.
  • Cached route and stop data for your city, so the App opens quickly.
  • On iPhone: commute reminders you create. They are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push server is involved.

This data is not sent to me. Deleting the App removes it. On Android, settings and favorites may be included in your device's Google backup if you have backups turned on.

Transit Data

The App downloads routes, stops, vehicle positions and arrival times from public transport services and open data feeds of each city. These requests contain the routes and stops you are viewing, but no personal data. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address.

Maps

The iOS version displays maps with Apple MapKit, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The Android version displays maps with the Google Maps SDK, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics & Crash Reports

Both versions use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. Firebase collects:

  • Anonymous usage events, such as which city is selected and which screens are opened.
  • Crash reports with technical details: device model, OS version, app version and the state of the App at the time of the crash.
  • A random app-instance identifier and general device information (model, OS, language, approximate country).

This data is not linked to your name or email. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Advertising

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and general device information to show and measure ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice later in the App's settings.
  • On iPhone, the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalized ads.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google's use of advertising data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-App Purchases

On iPhone, City Transport Pro is an optional subscription that removes ads and unlocks extra features. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and managed with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous customer identifier and your purchase history to confirm your subscription. I do not receive or store any payment information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

The Android version currently has no in-app purchases.

Information I Do Not Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account details
  • Your location history
  • Contacts, photos or other content on your device

Your Choices

  • Deny or revoke location access at any time; the App keeps working.
  • Change your ad consent in the App's settings, or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Delete the App to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Email me to ask about or delete any data related to you.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
