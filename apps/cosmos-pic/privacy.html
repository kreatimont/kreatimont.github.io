Privacy Policy

Applies to: Cosmos Pic

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Cosmos Pic ("the App") is developed and maintained by Alexandr Nadtoka ("I", "me", "my"). The App shows NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day for any date. This policy explains what data the App uses and the services it relies on.

1. The Short Version

  • No account or sign-in, and I don't ask for your name or email address.
  • The App does not access your location, contacts or photo library, except to save a photo when you ask it to.
  • The App uses Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reports, and includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in Section 3.
  • The App does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier.

2. NASA Photos

The App loads public images and descriptions from NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) API. These requests include the date you pick and standard technical information such as your IP address, handled by NASA according to the NASA Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

Provider Purpose Data involved
Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) Understanding how the App is used and fixing crashes A random app instance ID, app usage such as sessions and screens viewed, crash logs, device model, OS and app version, and approximate location derived from IP address. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
Google Mobile Ads SDK Advertising support When the App starts, the SDK may send device and app information, such as device model, OS version and IP address, to Google. The App does not request tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not available to it. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
Apple Inc. App distribution and optional crash reports If you share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymized crash and diagnostic reports. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

This data is not linked to your name or email address. I do not sell your personal information.

4. Saved Photos

If you save a photo, the App adds it to your photo library on your device. The App does not read your library or upload anything from it.

5. Contacting Support

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to respond to you.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact me.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Contact me at the address below. Google may process data in the United States under the safeguards described in its policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
