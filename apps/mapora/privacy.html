Privacy Policy

Applies to: Mapora

Effective Date: November 15, 2026  ·  Developer: Alexandr Nadtoka

Mapora ("the App") is a travel map and geography game app. It has no accounts and no backend of its own. This policy explains what stays on your device, what the App sends to the services it relies on, and the choices you have.

1. The Short Version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email address.
  • Your countries, cities, wishlist, game progress and maps are stored on your device. We can't see them.
  • Photo import runs entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and their locations are never uploaded.
  • The App uses Firebase for anonymous usage analytics, crash reports and remote settings, and RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, as described in Section 4. Analytics events contain counts and simple values, never the names of the countries or cities you've visited.
  • The App has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your travels: visited countries, cities, regions and US states, trip years and your wishlist.
  • Games: scores, streaks, XP, medals and achievements.
  • Maps you create and maps you've scanned from friends.
  • Settings and a cached copy of your Pro status.

We have no access to this data. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device settings.

3. Permissions

  • Photos (optional): if you allow access, the App reads the location and date stored in your photos to find the countries and cities you've visited. This happens on your device. Photos are not uploaded, and nothing is added to your map until you confirm it.
  • Location (optional): your approximate location is used on your device to suggest nearby medals and challenges and to help you mark where you are. It is not sent to us.
  • Camera (optional): used only to scan QR codes of shared maps.
  • Add to Photos (optional): used only to save shared map QR cards to your library.

Shared maps are encoded directly into the QR code. They are not uploaded to a server. Anyone who scans the code can see the map it contains.

4. Third-Party Services

Provider Purpose Data involved
Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) Understanding how the App is used, fixing crashes, and changing settings such as limits and experiments without an update A random app instance ID, events such as "onboarding completed" or "paywall shown" with counts and simple values (for example, how many countries were imported, but not which ones), coarse user properties such as Pro status and a bucketed country count, crash logs, device model, OS and app version, and approximate location derived from IP address. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
RevenueCat, Inc. Subscription management and purchase verification A random anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information, and basic technical information such as app version, device and operating system details, region and IP address. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
Apple Inc. App Store payments, maps and Look Around panoramas Apple processes your payment, and we never see your payment details. Map tiles and the 360° panoramas in "Where am I?" are loaded from Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
Wikimedia (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons) Country photos, coats of arms and descriptions Standard web requests for the images and articles you open, including your IP address. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

The App does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

5. Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device: deleting the App removes it. Copies in existing backups follow your backup settings.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase according to its retention settings and policies, and not linked to your name or email address.
  • Subscriptions: deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. You can cancel in your App Store account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no accounts, chat or ads. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Firebase and RevenueCat may process data in the United States under the safeguards described in their policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post changes here with a revised effective date.
