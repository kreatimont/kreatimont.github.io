FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does importing from photos work?

Mapora reads the location and date saved in your photos on your iPhone and groups them into the countries and cities you've visited. Nothing is uploaded. You review the list and choose what to add. Photos without location data, such as most screenshots and images saved from messages, can't be placed on the map.

Do I need Mapora Pro?

No. Marking countries, the globe, the games and the atlas are free. Mapora Pro adds unlimited cities, importing every city from your photos at once, and unlimited shared maps. If you already subscribed, open the paywall and tap Restore.

How do I share my map with a friend?

Create a map and share its QR code. Your friend scans it in Mapora to see your map. The map is stored inside the QR code itself, so it isn't uploaded anywhere.

Does Mapora work offline?

Your map, photo import and most games work offline. Where am I? panoramas, country photos and coats of arms need an internet connection to load.

Does Mapora collect any personal data?

Your travels, photos and progress stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage analytics and crash reports through Firebase, without the names of places you've visited, and uses RevenueCat for subscriptions. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or wrong data. How do I report it?

Email me at nadtoka.alexandr@gmail.com with what you found, and a screenshot if you can. A missing city or a wrong border is useful to know too.
