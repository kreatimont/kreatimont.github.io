Mapora

Light up every country you've been to, then learn the rest of the world by playing.

Overview Support Privacy Policy

About

A travel map for everywhere you've been

Watch the countries you've visited light up on a 3D globe. Go down to cities, regions and US states, let your photo library fill in the trips you forgot, and learn the rest of the world with daily geography games.

Mapora, the little globe buddy, guides you through the app and cheers you on in every game.

Features

What's Inside

Track your travels and play your way around the world.

🌍

Your World, Lit Up

Mark visited countries and your wishlist on a 3D globe or a flat map. See how many countries and continents you've seen, and add the year of each trip.

📸

Import From Photos

Mapora reads your photos' geotags on your iPhone and finds the countries and cities you've visited, with dates. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is added until you say yes.

📍

Beyond Countries

Track every city you visit. Regions and provinces fill in from your cities, every US state you've seen is colored on the map, and regions and cities show their coats of arms.

🎮

9 Geography Games

Flags, capitals, country outlines, Where am I? with 360° panoramas, Bigger or Smaller, City Population Battle, Match 4×4, timezones and more. Plus a daily challenge, streaks, XP and achievements.

🏅

Medals & Challenges

Earn medals for the regions of every country and take on challenges like Route 66 and the Alps.

📖

World Atlas

Every country with its flag, capital, population, region, photos and maps. Create your own maps and share them with friends by QR code.

Free to start

Countries, games and the atlas are free. Mapora Pro is an optional subscription for unlimited cities, importing every city from your photos at once, and unlimited shared maps.

Privacy

Your trips stay on your iPhone

No account. Your photos are scanned on your device and never uploaded, and your map stays on your iPhone. Mapora uses anonymous analytics and crash reports to improve the app.
